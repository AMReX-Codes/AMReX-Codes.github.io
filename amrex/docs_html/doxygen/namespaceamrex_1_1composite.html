Vector< Long > const & rank_offset,

SpMatrix< Real > & mat )
inline

Build an SpMatrix of this rank's rows [row_begin, row_begin+nrows) from (row, column, value) triplets in device memory, without copying them to the host: the device counterpart of exchange_triplets, OwnedCSR::define and OwnedCSR::toSpMatrix.

Triplets of rows owned by other ranks are sent to their owners through the host (collective over comm). The triplets of each row are sorted by (column, triplet index) and duplicates are summed in that order, so the values do not depend on thread scheduling. The triplet arrays are consumed.
