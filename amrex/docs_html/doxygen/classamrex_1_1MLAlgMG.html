Detailed Description

Algebraic system of one MG level of AMR level 0 of an MLLinOp, solved with AlgMG.

Built by MLLinOp::makeAlgMG under the ParallelContext communicator of that level. The matrix is assembled once; solve() can be called repeatedly.

Cell-centered operator alpha*a - beta*div(b grad): rows are all cells except those of fully covered boxes.

The a coefficient may be nullptr (zero); the b coefficients are required. eb_bcoef is the EB Dirichlet b coefficient or nullptr. bctype and bcl give the domain BC type and location per box and face, and maxorder the order of the Dirichlet boundary stencil (2 or 3).
